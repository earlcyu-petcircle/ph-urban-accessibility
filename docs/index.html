METRO MANILA URBAN ACCESSIBILITY

How accessible is your barangay to daily essentials?

Pick up to three Metro Manila barangays and compare how much of everyday life is a short walk from home. Check the walk to the nearest food shop, palengke, pharmacy, clinic, hospital, public school, open space and transit stop.

Pick up to three barangays

    Or click a barangay on the map.

    The examples are three barangays I have lived in: Barangay 136, Loyola Heights and Oranbo.

    Walks are along streets at 4.2 km/h. Minutes are for the median resident, the middle resident of the barangay by walk. Bold blue marks the best of the picks. Click a service name to show it on the map. Jollibee is shown like the other services but is not counted in the 15-minute score.

    Only the three example barangays have been through the study's ten sensitivity checks; small gaps between other picks may not hold.

    Data and methodology

    Sari-sari stores are almost unmapped, so food access is understated everywhere. Hexagons with fewer than five buildings are left off the map.
